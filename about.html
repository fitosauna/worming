About Worming
Founded 2023 in Auce, Latvia

Why we started.

Worming was founded in 2023 on a simple belief: good health starts with good food, and good food starts with healthy soil.

We care about nutrition, wellbeing and sustainable agriculture. Making worm humus is how we support the growers who produce high-quality organic food.

Family farming across generations.

Worming is built on practical farming knowledge passed from one generation to the next, combined with a modern approach to sustainable production and international business.

Made in Auce. Supplied to 15 countries.

Our production site is in Auce, Latvia. We manage the whole biohumus process there, from raw materials to finished product.
