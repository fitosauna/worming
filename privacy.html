Who we are

SIA "Worming", registration number 48503018418, "Līči", Vītiņu pagasts, Auces novads, LV-3721, Latvia, is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Contact: info@wormingbiohumuss.lv, +371 28643755.

What we collect

When you send a quote request we receive what you enter in the form: company, type of operation, country, delivery postcode or region, the product details you ask about, your name, work email, phone number if you give one, and your notes. If you email or call us, we keep that correspondence.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To prepare a quote and answer your request: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To deliver orders and keep accounting records: performance of a contract and legal obligations (Article 6(1)(b) and (c)).
  • To follow up on a business enquiry: our legitimate interest in running our business (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time.

We don't send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

Who receives it

Our email and form service providers, who process data for us, and carriers when we deliver an order. We don't sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Quote requests that don't lead to an order: up to two years after our last contact. Orders and invoices: as long as Latvian accounting law requires.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies and loads no third-party analytics. Fonts are served from our own site.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to info@wormingbiohumuss.lv. You can also complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, dvi.gov.lv).

Changes

We'll publish any changes on this page with a new date.
