Production, step by step.

  1. Feedstock

    Manure, vegetable matter and leaves come in and are prepared for the beds.

  2. Worm beds

    Californian red worms work through the material.

  1. Maturation

    The humus rests before it is harvested.

  2. Screening

    It is sieved to an even, fine crumb.

  1. Testing

    The material is checked against the specification.

  2. Packing

    It is filled, labelled and stored under cover until dispatch.
