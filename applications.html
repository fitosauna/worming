Where it fits in your operation.

Rates below are starting points, not prescriptions. Crops and systems differ, so confirm each one with a small trial.

Substrate producers Greenhouses and young plants Orchards and vineyards Organic vegetable growers

Substrate and growing-media producers

An organic component for potting and propagation mixes, including peat-reduced recipes.

Typical uses

Blended into finished growing media as one part of the recipe.

Starting rates

Start test batches at about 10% by volume and work up. Our own guidance for raising seedlings is 1 part worm humus to 3 parts peat or soil.

What to check

EC, pH and air capacity of the final blend. Our data sheet pH (KCl) is 7.3.

Evidence

Trial results coming soon.

Greenhouses and young plants

For propagation, potting on and planting out under glass or plastic.

Typical uses

Sowing and pricking-out mixes for cucumbers, tomatoes, peppers and brassicas. In the planting hole at transplanting.

Starting rates

Seedling mixes: up to 1 part to 3 parts peat or soil. Tomatoes and cucumbers: about 100 g in each planting hole, then water.

What to check

High mix rates can raise EC. Check salinity, and trial first on sub-irrigated benches.

Evidence

Trial results coming soon.

Orchards and vineyards

Mixed into the planting hole for new trees, vines and berry crops.

Typical uses

New plantings of fruit trees and berry bushes. Monthly top-dressing around established plants in the growing season.

Starting rates

Apple, pear, cherry and plum: 1.5 to 2 kg per planting hole. Currants and gooseberries: 1 to 1.5 kg. Strawberries: 150 to 200 g per plant. For vines, ask us and plan a trial row.

What to check

Mix it with the soil in the hole instead of leaving it in a layer, and water after planting.

Evidence

Trial results coming soon.

Organic vegetable growers

Bed preparation, transplanting and feeding through the season, in tunnels and in the field.

Typical uses

Worked into beds before sowing or planting. Placed under transplants. Top-dressed during growth.

Starting rates

Onions, carrots and beets: 0.5 kg per m². Salad, dill and parsley: 1 L per m² of row. Potatoes: about 50 g under each plant. In season: about 1 L per m² once a month, worked in and watered.

What to check

Vermicompost is authorised for EU organic production (Regulation (EU) 2021/1165, Annex II). Confirm with your certifier.

Run a trial before you order.

The rates on this page follow the recommendations of the Latvian Biohumus Producers' Association. Your own side-by-side trial tells you more than any table.

  1. Pick a small area

    One bay, a few rows or one substrate batch.

  2. Keep a control

    Leave an untreated strip right next to it.

  3. Measure

    Record rooting, growth and EC for four to six weeks.

  4. Decide

    Send us the results and we'll talk through a rate for your system.
