Worm humus, screened and tested.

Biohumus (vermicompost)
Processed organic soil improver

The material.

Feedstock

Organic matter: manure, vegetable matter and leaves.

Worms

Californian red worms eat through the feedstock. What they leave behind is the product.

Result

A dark material that looks like black earth and has no smell. It contains plant nutrients and humic substances.

How it differs from compost.

CompostWorm humus
Made byMicrobes, in a heap that heats upWorms, at ambient temperature
TextureVaries with the source materialFine and earth-like
Used asBulk organic matter, by the tonneA small share of a mix or planting hole

They do different jobs. Most growers who use worm humus keep using compost too.
